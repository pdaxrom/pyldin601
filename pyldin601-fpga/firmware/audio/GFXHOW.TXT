PYLDIN GFXMUSIC - HAM8 / SPRITES / AY DEMO

GFXMUSIC song.YM|PSG|VTX [/L] [/A|C|S|M]
B:GFXMUSIC B:DEMOH5.YM /L
HG: B:HG, then E:GFXMUSIC E:LEDSTRM1.YM /L
Use the drive letter reported by HG.
SPACE pauses/resumes music and animation; ESC returns to UniDOS.
ESC also cancels loading and graphics preparation.

Needs HD6303, paged RAM and the current graphics v5/audio FPGA.
Both models 601/601A need updated ROM with the INT E5 RAM manager.
The current graphics v5/audio FPGA needs no update.
8 MHz is recommended for preparation; playback also works at 1 MHz.
Conventional RAM: about 28 KiB plus DOS buffers.
Music is decoded by the native YPLAY engine into up to 888 KiB.
Only song-sized banks are allocated; other RAM owners are retained.
See YPLAYHOW.TXT for original YM/PSG/VTX/LH5 formats and clocks.

Three transparent round sprites move across a procedural HAM8
background. All four screen edges are clipped by the blitter.
Only old sprite rectangles are restored; drawing is on the hidden
frame page and FLIP waits for VBL. SPACE freezes both parts.
Background colours reset every four pixels; horizontal sprite
positions and opaque runs use that grid to avoid HAM colour trails.
This restriction belongs to this demo, not the general COPY command.

Graphics pages 0/1: double buffer; 2: atlas; 3: static background.
These occupy 256 KiB and do not overlap the 888-KiB music banks.
The demo replaces these graphics pages and its allocated music RAM.
Song banks are released on EOF, ESC, errors and DOS termination.
On exit: full PAL palette, INT E4, AY clock/timer and page registers
are restored; audio is silent and UniDOS text geometry is retained.
Build GFXMUSIC.ASM with real UniAS and -D GFXMUSIC_DEMO.
