GFX.PGM: 320x200 RGB332 / 256 fixed colours, for either 601 or 601A.
Run B:\GFX. SPACE switches between the palette and the hardware-copy frame.
ESC restores the native video mode and returns to UniDOS.
Choose any CPU frequency in BIOS; 8 MHz makes command submission faster.
The blitter and display also work at 1 MHz without HOLD.
The demo uses SRAM pages 0 and 1 of the graphics extension (100000..11FFFF).
It overwrites those graphics pages, preserving base RAM, ROM and RAM disk.
